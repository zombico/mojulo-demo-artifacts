XR voice test v4 — talk to the room

TEST PAGE ONLY — not Maison3D yet. This page proves one thing: can you talk, in the headset, and change this room? Voice here is handled locally in the page — nothing is sent to Muse yet, and no server is involved. If this works, the next step is wiring the same trigger into Maison3D's loop. If speech recognition is missing in the Quest browser, that itself is the proof, and typing is the fallback.

Open directly in the native Meta Browser on the Quest. First, on this 2D page, press Hold to talk once: it asks for the microphone and shows whether this browser can recognise speech. Then tap ENTER VR, and in VR hold a controller trigger, speak, and let go.

Try saying: “make the sofa red”, “make the cube blue”, “make the table bigger”, “move the cube left”, “spin the cube”, “hide the lamp”, “show the lamp”.
